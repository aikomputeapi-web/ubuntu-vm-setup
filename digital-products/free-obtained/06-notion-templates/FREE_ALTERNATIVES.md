# Notion Templates — Free Alternatives to 24 Etsy Listings

All 24 scraped Etsy Notion template listings have **free equivalents**. Notion's own
official template gallery is free, and building a custom template inside Notion requires 
zero tooling — just a Notion account (free tier suffices).

## How to Use These Free Alternatives

### Option A: Use Notion's Official Free Templates
1. Go to [notion.com/templates](https://www.notion.com/templates)
2. Browse by category (Life Planner, Student, Business, ADHD, etc.)
3. Click "Duplicate" to add any template to your workspace — free forever
4. Customize to your needs

### Option B: Build Your Own (30 minutes, no code)
1. Create a new page in Notion
2. Add databases (tables, boards, calendars)
3. Add linked views and dashboards
4. Use formulas for automation
5. Share > Publish as template — you now have a reusable template

### Option C: Use Third-Party Free Template Galleries
- [Notionland](https://www.notionland.co/templates) — free community templates
- [Notion Everything](https://www.notioneverything.com) — searchable template database
- [Notionable](https://notionable.com) — curated free templates

## Quick Build: "Second Brain" Notion Template (DIY in 10 minutes)

1. **Create databases:**
   - Tasks (with Status, Priority, Due Date, Project properties)
   - Notes (with Tags, Date, Source properties)
   - Projects (with Status, Timeline, Related Tasks)
   - Goals (with Progress bar, Milestones)

2. **Create dashboard page:**
   - Add linked views from each database
   - Filter: "This Week" tasks, "Active" projects, "Recent" notes

3. **Add navigation:**
   - Page links in a sidebar callout block
   - Quick-add buttons with template buttons

4. **Publish as template:**
   - Share > Publish to web > "Allow duplicate as template"
   - Share the link — anyone can duplicate your template for free
