## Important: PLR/MRR Reality Check

### What these Etsy sellers actually do:
1. Download free PLR products from the sources above
2. Rezip them with new cover art
3. List on Etsy with inflated quantity claims ("85 Million!")
4. Add PLR/MRR licence terms (which they got free)

### The actual content is:
- Recycled articles from 2010-2020
- Public domain art scans
- Free SVG files from CC0 sources
- Canva Free templates repackaged
- Old ebooks with new covers

### Recommendation:
**Don't buy PLR/MRR bundles on Etsy.** Go directly to the free sources above
and get the identical content. Or better yet, create your own original products
using the tools and APIs documented in the other folders here.

### If you want to SELL PLR/MRR products:
1. Create genuinely original content (planners, templates, graphics)
2. Package with clear licence terms
3. Price based on quality, not inflated quantity claims
4. Use our DIY tools (planner generator, SVG generator, etc.) to create products
   at zero marginal cost
