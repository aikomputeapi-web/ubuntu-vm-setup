# Canva Templates — Free Alternatives to 23 Etsy Listings

Canva templates on Etsy are marked "Partial" for free equivalence: they're free to
*use* via Canva's free tier, but Canva's licence terms block *reselling* their stock
templates as your own. The DIY route — designing your own templates in Canva — is
the clean path for both personal use and resale.

## How to Get Free Canva Templates

### Option A: Use Canva's Free Template Gallery
1. Go to [canva.com/templates](https://www.canva.com/templates)
2. Filter by "Free" (Canva labels free templates with a free tag)
3. Use the template for personal projects at no cost
4. For resalable products: design your own layouts using free/licensed elements

### Option B: Design Your Own Templates in Canva (Free)
1. Create a free Canva account at [canva.com](https://www.canva.com)
2. Choose a template size (Instagram Post, Carousel, Business Card, etc.)
3. Use free elements (search "free" in the elements panel)
4. Design your layout
5. Share > "Template link" — anyone who opens it gets your layout as a template

### Option C: Use Free Alternative Tools
- [Photopea](https://www.photopea.com) — free Photoshop alternative for template design
- [Figma](https://www.figma.com) — free for individuals, exports to PNG/PDF/SVG
- [Piktochart](https://piktochart.com) — free tier for infographics
- [Snappa](https://snappa.com) — free tier for social media graphics

## DIY: How to Create Saleable Canva Templates (No Pro Required)

1. **Use Canva Free tier** — create designs using only free elements
2. **Design original layouts** — don't copy Canva's stock templates
3. **Export as template link** — Share > Template Link
4. **Sell the link** — buyers get a one-click copy to their Canva account
5. **License your work** — add your own MRR/PLR terms if reselling

### Canva Licence Warning
Canva's Content Licence Agreement prohibits:
- Reselling Canva's stock templates AS templates
- Reselling Canva's stock photos/elements as standalone files
- Claiming Canva-owned content as your own

**Safe path:** Design your own layouts using free/licensed elements. Your original
layout design is your intellectual property and can be sold freely.
